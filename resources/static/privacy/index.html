Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This is a small personal website. I use limited analytics to understand whether the site works, which parts are useful, and when something breaks. I do not sell your personal information.

What this site collects

When you visit, the site may automatically collect basic technical and usage information, including:

  • the page you viewed and the referring page;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • browser, device, operating system, and user-agent information;
  • an anonymous visitor or session identifier; and
  • interactions such as clicking the email or LinkedIn links, downloading the résumé, and technical errors.

The site does not have a payment form or contact form. Accounts are handled by KT Accounts, which is described in its own section below. If you email me, your message is handled by your email provider and mine, not by the website analytics described here.

PostHog analytics

This site uses PostHog for product analytics and error diagnostics. Analytics requests are sent through a custom aidenkt.com endpoint and processed using PostHog’s service.

PostHog may use cookies or similar browser storage to distinguish visits and sessions. The information sent to PostHog can include the technical and interaction data listed above and, if enabled in the analytics configuration, a replay of on-page interactions. PostHog processes that information on my behalf so I can measure site usage and diagnose problems.

How the information is used

I use this information only to:

  • understand general traffic and how visitors use the site;
  • measure whether links and downloads are useful;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse; and
  • find and fix errors.

KT Accounts

KT Accounts is the sign-in service for my sites. It is not open for sign-up. New accounts are by invitation only.

If you have an account, it stores:

  • the name, email address, and profile picture address that Google or GitHub provided when the account was created;
  • the sign-in methods you use and, for Google and GitHub, the ID that service gives your account and the permissions you granted;
  • your passkeys, the names you give them, and technical details such as the kind of authenticator;
  • your sign-in sessions, with the IP address and browser information for each;
  • your display name, handle, profile picture, and banner;
  • the permissions on your account, any invitations you create, and any agents you set up, including the requests they make for access; and
  • a log of account events, such as sign-ins and changes to the account, with the IP address and browser information of each request, which are deleted after 90 days.

When you sign in with Google or GitHub, that service sends me your name, email address, profile picture, and account ID. For GitHub, this means permission to read your profile and email addresses. I use this information only to create your account, sign you in, and show your name and email address back to you. I do not request access to anything else in your Google or GitHub account, and I do not store access tokens from either service. Information from Google is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than Cloudflare, which hosts the service.

KT Accounts uses cookies to keep you signed in, to complete a sign-in, and to hold an invitation while you create your account. It does not load analytics or third-party scripts. Cloudflare keeps a log of requests to the service, which I use to find and fix errors. Whether or not you have an account, your IP address is kept for up to two days to prevent abuse.

Profile pictures and banners can be viewed by anyone who has the link to them. When you are signed in and visit one of my other sites, that site can read your account ID, display name, handle, picture, and permissions to show you as signed in. Your email address is not sent to those sites.

You can delete your account yourself in your account settings. Deleting the account removes the information above, except the log of account events, which is kept, and the handle, which stays reserved for 30 days. For any other account request, email support@aidenkt.com.

Sharing and retention

Analytics data is shared with PostHog as the service provider that processes it. Account data is stored with Cloudflare as the service provider that hosts KT Accounts. I do not sell or rent this data. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the site, its visitors, or others.

Data is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, subject to the retention settings available through PostHog. Sign-in sessions end after 7 days. Other account data is kept until the account is deleted, except for the log of account events and the reserved handle described above. You may contact me to ask about or request deletion of information associated with you. Because most analytics are collected without your name, I may not always be able to connect an anonymous record to you.

Your choices

You can limit analytics storage by blocking cookies or site data in your browser, using a privacy or content-blocking extension, or clearing stored site data. Blocking analytics should not prevent you from reading the site.

Links to other services

This site links to services such as LinkedIn and your email application, and KT Accounts sends you to Google or GitHub if you choose to sign in with them. Their privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@aidenkt.com.

02 · Logo

The KT mark

  • One block, three pieces. A bar, a K that turns into a T, and one square sitting off to the top right.
  • The square is the unit. Every stroke is two squares wide and every gap is about one.
  • Give it room. Leave one stroke of empty space on every side. That's the dashed frame.
  • One color at a time. Any color from the palette works, as long as it's only one.

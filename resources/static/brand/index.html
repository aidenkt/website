02 · Logo

The KT logo

  • Three shapes. The logo is a bar, a K that turns into a T, and a small block in the top right corner.
  • How it's measured. Everything is measured off that small block. The strokes are two blocks wide and the gaps are about one.
  • Space around it. Please leave at least one stroke of empty space on every side. That's what the dashed frame is showing.
  • One color. The whole logo should be a single color. Any color from the palette works.
